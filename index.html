Missing Piece

Real-time co-op study game for 2–8 players

Everyone joins from their own phone and gets different fact cards for the same question. Nobody can answer alone, so the team shares what they know, votes in secret and agrees before time runs out. The design is based on the jigsaw method teachers use to get every student participating: each needed card rules out at least one wrong answer.

  • Real-time multiplayer over WebSockets on Cloudflare Workers, with one Durable Object per room (SQLite-backed) as the single source of truth. Each player only ever receives their own cards, and answers stay hidden until the reveal.
  • Built for real phones: automatic reconnect, a 60-second grace period that keeps your cards, a 10-minute seat hold, late joiners and host hand-over. Includes solo mode with two bot teammates and custom question packs with validation.
  • Game rules live in game.mjs, separate from networking in worker.mjs, and are covered by game-flow tests on Node’s built-in test runner.
  • Built in four days with ChatGPT (Codex) for the Handshake AI Skills Studio x OpenAI Multiplayer Game Challenge. I wrote the requirements, approved the plan, tested every milestone across phones and browsers, and directed each fix.
  • Playtesting drove the design: after a first game where nobody shared cards (2/6), I added one-tap sharing and moved chat under the cards; by the third playtest the team scored 4/6.
